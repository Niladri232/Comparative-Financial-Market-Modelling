
RANDOM WALK SIMULATION - NIFTY 50
=================================

Dataset:
NIFTY 50 daily adjusted closing prices
Period: 04 January 2010 - 11 September 2026

Simulation:
Gaussian Random Walk with drift
Number of simulations: 1000
Trading days per simulation: 4100
Random seed: 42

Estimated Parameters:
Daily mean return: 0.00036532
Daily volatility: 0.01039576
Annualized return: 9.2061%
Annualized volatility: 16.5028%

Main Findings:
1. Simulated mean is close to the actual mean.
2. Simulated standard deviation is close to actual volatility.
3. Actual returns exhibit negative skewness.
4. Actual returns exhibit substantial excess kurtosis.
5. Gaussian simulations do not reproduce the observed
   heavy tails and negative skewness.
6. Actual extreme returns exceed the simulated sample
   extremes in magnitude.
7. Simulated terminal prices have a wide, right-skewed
   distribution.

Conclusion:
The Gaussian Random Walk provides a useful baseline for
describing the average return and unconditional volatility
of NIFTY 50. However, it fails to reproduce important
distributional characteristics of real financial returns,
particularly negative skewness and excess kurtosis.

Further modelling is required to investigate heavy tails,
volatility clustering and changing market conditions.
